Method. The 9 AM price is the open of the first candle at or after 9:00:00 AM in New York (EDT in summer, EST in winter; each row shows which). The end price is the open of the candle at that hour. Midnight means 12:00 AM Monday.

Sources. BTC/USD: Bitstamp 1-minute candles (a true USD market). SOL/USD: Binance spot SOL/USDT hourly candles; USDT tracks the dollar to within a few hundredths of a percent. Both come from public GitHub datasets refreshed by GitHub Actions, because exchange APIs were not reachable when this was built.

Approximate rows (≈). Sunday Sep 6, 2026 for SOL uses hourly CoinGecko price snapshots taken about 25 minutes past each hour, because the Binance file had not reached that day yet. Its sign was negative at every end hour regardless of which nearby snapshot is used as the 9 AM reference. The 8 PM snapshot was missing, so that cell is blank.
